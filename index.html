Privacy Policy – Beginner Workout

Last Updated: August 28, 2026

Beginner Workout ("the App") is an offline workout application designed to help users follow and manage beginner exercise routines.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Beginner Workout application.

Important: Beginner Workout is designed to work offline. The App does not require users to create an account or provide personal contact information.

1. Information We Collect

Beginner Workout does not require you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical address to use the App.

The App contains pre-filled exercise and workout information. Users may also add, modify, or delete exercise and workout information within the App.

Workout-related information may include:

  • Exercises included in workout routines
  • Exercise completion or progress information
  • Workout preferences and settings
  • Exercise or workout information added by the user

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Workout and exercise information created or modified within the App is stored locally on your device.

This information is used only to provide the features and functionality of the Beginner Workout application.

The App does not transmit your saved workout information to our servers.

3. Adding and Deleting Exercises

The App includes pre-filled exercises. After installing the App, you may add new exercises or workout information according to the features available in the App.

You may delete exercises or workout information that you have added whenever the corresponding delete functionality is available.

4. Deleting Your Data

You can delete saved workout or exercise information using the deletion options available inside the App.

You can also remove locally stored App data through your Android device settings by clearing the App's storage/data or by uninstalling the App.

Once locally stored information is deleted, it may not be recoverable.

5. Internet Connection

The core functionality of Beginner Workout is designed to work offline.

An internet connection is not required to access the main workout features of the App.

6. Third-Party Services

Beginner Workout does not sell or share your locally stored workout information with third parties.

The current version of the App does not transmit saved workout information to external servers.

If future versions introduce third-party services or online features, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain how information is handled.

7. Children's Privacy

Beginner Workout does not require users to create an account or provide personal contact information.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Security

Workout information is stored locally on your device. We recommend using the security features provided by your device to help protect locally stored information.

However, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if the App's functionality or data practices change.

Any updated Privacy Policy will be published at this URL.
